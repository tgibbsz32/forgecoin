# ForgeCoin Community Code of Conduct

ForgeCoin contributors and community members must communicate respectfully, discuss technical disagreements with evidence, avoid harassment and discrimination, and never pressure others to reveal wallet, identity, or financial information.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove abusive or dangerous content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers whenever possible.
