# mFRG proposed token sale — preliminary U.S. legal risk review

**Prepared October 28, 2026. Status: PRELIMINARY DESK REVIEW — NOT LEGAL CLEARANCE.**
Scope: U.S.-facing sales, with a potential South Carolina nexus. Foreign jurisdictions, exact issuer location/entity, purchasers, marketing and custody arrangements remain unresolved. No sale, funds acceptance, investment solicitation or launch is authorized. This memorandum is not legal advice, an attorney-client relationship, a legal opinion, or a substitute for retained qualified securities, payments/BSA, tax and consumer counsel.

## Executive finding — HIGH / DO NOT LAUNCH

The intended **5M mFRG pilot** at five USD-equivalent price tiers ($0.08/$0.10/$0.12/$0.14/$0.16), **$600,000 maximum gross if sold out**, and explicit 20% ($120k) gross-founder-proceeds target present material legal and reputational risks. mFRG is **not yet minted on Solana mainnet**, material utilities and liquidity are under development, and value expectations can depend on promoter execution. Marketing successive increasing prices, planned liquidity and staking creates a **significant possibility that the sale constitutes an investment-contract offering** even if the asset's code is otherwise not itself a security. The project cannot lawfully rely on calling the token a "utility token" or saying "not an investment."

The SEC's April 2026 discussion describes the Howey analysis: money investment, common enterprise, reasonable expectation of profits from essential managerial efforts. The SEC's August 2026 Regulation Crypto Assets was **proposed**, not a self-executing exemption. An applicable registered or valid exempt offering pathway must be determined **before marketing or selling** if this is a securities transaction.

## Required offering documentation

At minimum, after counsel selects the lawful pathway: identity and jurisdiction of issuer; token's status and utility *as deployed*; signed code/audit results; 500M allocation table; vesting and insider control; precise pilot tiers and maximum rather than forecast proceeds; authorized payment types; all costs; treasury custody and audit rights; related-party compensation; conflicts; refund/cancellation terms; use-of-proceeds and accounting policy; risk of total loss; no guarantee of markets or liquidity; risk that future upgrades are delayed or never built; AML/OFAC/privacy terms; tax notices; purchaser suitability or eligibility; and transaction-specific mandatory filings.

Founder proceeds must be disclosed with precise **gross-versus-net** accounting. The $600k figure is a **sell-out target**, not money already received. $120k founder proceeds is a **total intended share of gross**, not $120k upfront. The old planning notes' 80% liquidity/network and 20% founders allocation should specify operating and liquidity budgeting separately before public fund collection. If sale receipts are less than the maximum, a 20% share yields less than $120k; the project must not promise the founder a guaranteed fixed payment from buyer funds.
