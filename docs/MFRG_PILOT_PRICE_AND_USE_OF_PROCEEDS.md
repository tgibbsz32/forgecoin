# miniFORGE — Adopted Pilot Sale Pricing and Allocation Purposes

**Policy decision: 2026-10-28.** The project's intended price ladder is fixed for *planning and implementation specifications*, subject to lawful offering review, deployment acceptance and final owner authorization. Fixed policy figures do **not** guarantee that tokens sell, obligate buyers, establish fair value, or create a currently open offering.

Gross figures assume full sell-through, no refunds and no fees. No sale is open. Prices are USD-equivalent planning figures, not SOL quotes. Proposed payment instruments USDC and SOL require independent exchange-rate, settlement, legal and smart-contract approvals.

**Proposed use of fully collected $600,000 gross proceeds:** $480,000 (80%) to liquidity/network development and operations; **$120,000 (20%) founder proceeds** with **$60,000 immediate** and **$60,000 over six months** under the prior 50/50 payout design. The $120,000 is a **total** founder proceeds target, not $120,000 immediately. Taxes, external fees, refunds and legal limitations may change actual distributable net amounts. Founder proceeds are separate from the founder's token allocations, must be prominently disclosed before any offering, and require lawful approval and transparent financial reporting. These proceeds are external assets, not extra mFRG issuance. No contract or transfer is authorized here.

The pilot comprises 5M of the 50M **lifetime** fundraising treasury. The remaining 45M is allocated to staged later windows (15M additional Year 1; 20M Years 2–3; 10M Years 4–10), **without fixed future sale prices**. Any future sale price requires its own policy decision.

## Availability and risk notice

The pricing ladder is a project policy, **not an investment forecast** or claim that market demand supports a $40M–$80M implied fully diluted valuation. Actual circulating supply, market trading price, liquidity and ability to sell tokens are unknown. Early purchasers may face substantial market risk, liquidity risk, centralization and related-party conflicts. No returns, exchanges, funding level or launch dates are promised. Sales require independent legal, consumer protection, sanctions/AML and tax review and explicit owner launch authorization. **Never send funds to an unsolicited address.**
