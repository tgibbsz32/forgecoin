## Owner decisions confirmed — 2026-10-24

> **Current owner direction (2026-10-24):** Restored native-only fair launch: 50M FRG primary-emission reference, no allocated premine, 100% PoW miner subsidy and perpetual 0.20 FRG/block tail. The 55M/5M genesis treasury proposal is withdrawn. Native trading and user-deposit-backed Solana interoperability are future research. See [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md).


**Genesis:** Retain the existing candidate genesis output *only if independent cryptographic and consensus review proves it permanently unspendable*. Until then, neither the current transaction nor any replacement is approved for release. No allocated founder/team/ICO premine is authorized. Failure to prove permanent unspendability requires renewed explicit owner approval of a replacement design and repeat of genesis-dependent tests.

**Monetary policy:** Intended issuance is 100% of native block subsidy to PoW miners, with no ICO or founder/team premine, a **50,000,000 FRG primary-emission reference (not a hard cap)**, and a perpetual **0.20 FRG/block tail reward**, conditional on exact release-code and economics audit. Fees are paid under native consensus; this statement does not grant treasury or governance mint authority.

**Network/difficulty:** The fresh candidate's distinct identity and ports, initial difficulty 500,000, floor 400,000, LWMA activation from height 16 and 90-interval window remain **provisional**, subject to full-height, ARM64 and adversarial testing. They are not activated on the existing public development chain.

**Ten-seat governance model for design review:** Revisit the historical 10-seat architecture using native FRG. A seat represents one vote. No seats, founder voting rights, thresholds or voter identities are ratified yet. Miner-constituency qualification would require independently verified native mining plus an enforceably locked native FRG governance bond; neither verification nor locking is implemented. No seat may be allocated solely according to wealth without a reviewed and ratified process. Proposed ten-seat allocations, founder participation, term lengths, quorum, ties and veto/safeguards are awaiting owner review; practice elections are nonbinding.

**Existing holders:** The existing public development chain and proposed fresh mainnet are **distinct networks**. There will be **no automatic balance migration, exchange of old FRG for new FRG, snapshot-based allocation or transfer of wallets/coin balances**. Any future recognition arrangement would require a separate published and approved proposal; nothing is promised. Publish a clear announcement so miners, users, exchanges and pools do not confuse network identities.

This owner-approved direction does not itself alter network code, prove genesis unspendability, ratify governance or authorize mainnet launch.
