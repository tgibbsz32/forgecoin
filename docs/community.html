02 / GOVERNANCE

Representation and accountability.

The project retains a ten-seat chamber as its native FRG governance design target. Each seat represents one vote; constituency allocation and election safeguards still require ratification. Miner representatives must qualify through verified mining and enforceable native FRG bonding. Neither mining power nor a large wallet balance alone grants unilateral authority.

How a proposal becomes policy

  1. Public proposal and impact disclosures
  2. Open discussion and eligibility review
  3. Review proposed representation and voting safeguards
  4. Ratify procedures before any binding elections
  5. Publish implementation and adoption requirements
  6. Audited execution and published records

No binding quorum, voting threshold or schedule is ratified. Native consensus changes require independently reviewed releases and voluntary node adoption.

Miner qualifications without Solana staking.

The proposed miner representative will require independently verified native FRG mining contribution and an enforceable native FRG governance bond maintained through the term.
