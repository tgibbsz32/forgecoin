02 / GOVERNANCE

Ten seats. Equal chamber votes.

Stake determines eligibility and voting influence in elections for the five staking representatives; it does not multiply a representative's vote once seated. Constituencies choose their own delegates under separate eligibility criteria.

How a proposal becomes policy

  1. Public proposal and impact disclosures
  2. Open discussion and eligibility review
  3. Seven-day chamber vote
  4. Stakeholder ratification where required
  5. Enforced notice and timelock
  6. Audited execution and published records

Draft thresholds: 8 of 10 council votes for protected monetary amendments, plus qualified staker ratification; minimum 90 days' monetary-policy notice.

03 / ELECTIONS

Representative elections

The first readiness review is proposed for 90 days after a future public launch, with additional checks every 30 days. No election date is scheduled and registration is not open.

01 — RegisterVerify Solana wallet control and community eligibility
02 — QualifyVerify stake, mining work, development or community history
03 — VoteParticipate through an independently verifiable ballot
04 — AuditPublish results, challenges and representative mandates

Potential eligibility evidence: GitHub contributions, mining proofs, established community participation and optional LinkedIn/social history. A social account is not sufficient proof of unique identity.

04 / wFRG STAKING

Mining-funded staking. No promised yield.

Not operational

Staking bank — design preview

Eligible FRG rewards would authorize 0.5 wFRG per FRG beginning at native block 60. Ninety percent would go to mining recipients and ten percent to a staking-reward vault.

Safety before deposits

Staking must eventually use an independently tested Solana program and user-signed transactions, with published program IDs, withdrawal rules, account permissions and audited rewards.

No working Connect Wallet or Stake button is presented here. This prevents an informational site from being mistaken for an operational financial service.
