02 / GOVERNANCE

Rules before authority.

Before any authorized mFRG distribution begins, the project intends to select and publish governance rules. They should specify who may propose and approve spending, how community participation is established, conflict-of-interest handling, appeals, treasury signing thresholds, safeguards and implementation boundaries. No rights have been issued yet.

Proposed decision process

  1. Publish possible governance structures and eligibility policies
  2. Solicit miner, contributor, holder and public feedback
  3. Review treasury risk, key custody and operational safeguards
  4. Publish and ratify final rules through a process selected and disclosed in advance
  5. Operate, audit and publish decisions only after security and authorization checks

A project vote cannot automatically change native FRG consensus. Full node owners decide which network software to run.
