Potential future mining-security research

The selected FRG consensus is independent RandomX; merged mining is not enabled. Optional future proposals include FRG-parent / Tari-auxiliary mining, subject to a patched proxy and proof-compatibility verification, and separately Monero-parent / FRG-auxiliary mining, which would change FRG consensus and require a coordinated reviewed hard fork. Neither proposal brings automatic security or guaranteed extra miner rewards. Independent miner distribution, monitoring of competing chain tips and persistent reorganization-related payout holds are the immediate security focus. Security and merged-mining engineering plan.

Separate miniFORGE SPL plans

miniFORGE (mFRG) is not native FRG. Its proposed 50M mFRG fixed supply includes 5M initial presale, 5M future liquidity and 40M combined ecosystem reserve. The proposed flat sale price is $0.12 per token, with gross proceeds allocated 90% project / 10% founders (half founder proceeds immediately and half over six months). This is not a live sale. Native FRG remains separate. The intended later mFRG utility includes a fully native-FRG-reserved 1 FRG : 1 eligible mFRG bridge for faster Solana transfers, supported DEXs, optional finite-reserve staking and optional payments for USD-priced CITY packs in ForgeCoin's planned Discover Your City game. Every redeemable mFRG must have one independently verified FRG reserve; the fixed 50M mFRG supply caps capacity despite ongoing FRG tail issuance. No backing, bridge, mint, staking, DEX market or game checkout is active. Current mFRG policy.

A governance solution is intended to be selected and published before mFRG is introduced, minted, sold, bridged, staked or distributed. No fixed chamber or voter rights are approved. Future FRG privacy research includes FCMP++/CARROT and potential L1/L2/L3 projects, with no preapproved activation.
