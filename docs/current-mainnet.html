Separate miniFORGE SPL plans

miniFORGE (mFRG) is not native FRG. Its 50M target Solana standard SPL distribution comprises 5M for an initial future sale, 5M for a separate post-sale liquidity/staking treasury, and 40M for future issuance and development. No mainnet token sale, price, staking contract or guaranteed conversion is active.

A governance solution is intended to be selected and published before the mFRG sale concludes. No fixed chamber or voter rights are approved. Future FRG privacy research includes FCMP++/CARROT and potential L1/L2/L3 projects, with no preapproved activation.
