Discover Your City × miniFORGE

A persistent multiplayer city simulation built around real-world geography, property discovery, virtual ownership, city economies and social exploration.

Development concept — not a live token checkout. CITY is an in-game currency; mFRG is a separate proposed Solana SPL asset. No mainnet mint, game purchase integration, or presale is confirmed operational.

Explore real places

Walk or drive through geographically grounded cities, discover neighborhoods and properties, and earn eligible movement and exploration rewards subject to verification.

Shared multiplayer economy

Build property portfolios, manage upgrades and businesses, and interact with other players in a persistent world with server-authoritative ownership records.

Use mFRG optionally

Players may eventually purchase USD-priced CITY packs using mFRG alongside other supported payment methods. Quoted mFRG amounts would adjust with verified exchange rates.

How the proposed mFRG payment works

  1. Select a CITY pack priced in USD.
  2. Choose optional mFRG checkout and review a time-limited quote and fees.
  3. Send the exact mFRG amount to a verified project-controlled recipient.
  4. Server validates the finalized Solana transaction and prevents replay or duplicate credit.
  5. Only after successful verification, the game credits CITY; 100% of received mFRG is accounted to the designated ecosystem treasury.

Wallet screenshots or client-side claims never count as payment. Refund, custody, legal and platform-policy requirements must be resolved before enabling purchases.

Token supply

The revised planning target is 50,000,000 mFRG: 5M presale, 5M future liquidity and 40M combined staking/collateral/ecosystem reserve. Presale price and reserve suballocations are not yet approved. Read the revised policy.

Separate from native ForgeCoin

Native FRG is mined on the ForgeCoin chain. mFRG is a distinct Solana asset. Any future FRG-to-mFRG bridge or 1:1 backing requires independent engineering, reserves and audit; it is not available today.
