SOLANA · MINI FORGE

Future mFRG exchange concept — not guaranteed

Concept-only mFRG exchange and Solana utility, subject to native FRG verification and published governance before mFRG introduction. Intended features are faster Solana transfers, supported DEX trading, optional staking and a fully native-FRG-backed 1:1 conversion bridge. Implementation is not guaranteed. No wallet connection, token sale, liquidity or swap is enabled.

Acquire mFRG

Trading not enabled. The official mint, liquidity or treasury-sale contract, and independent launch checks have not been verified or published.

You receivemFRG — unavailable

Never send tokens to an address displayed outside an authenticated, audited transaction flow. Do not treat this preview as an offer to sell tokens. Solana token transfers are publicly observable and do not inherit native FRG privacy.

How trading will work

Solana: supported wallets approve USDC/SOL trades through a verified on-chain liquidity route or a reviewed treasury-sale program. Ethereum: users approve an ETH/USDC cross-chain route that delivers mFRG on Solana, with a clear fee and finality disclosure. Quote, minimum received, expiration, recipient and total charges must be displayed before signing.

Native FRG and miniFORGE (mFRG) remain distinct chain assets, but ForgeCoin intends a 1 FRG : 1 eligible mFRG reserve-backed bridge for Solana-side transfers and DEX use. It would require one independently verified native FRG in custody per redeemable mFRG—including presale or DEX inventory described as backed—and audited lock/release and redemption; no such bridge exists today. The proposed mFRG supply is 50M (5M presale, 5M liquidity and 40M combined staking/collateral/ecosystem). Native FRG tail issuance means mFRG has limited bridge capacity. A numerical 1:1 bridge target is not a fixed DEX market price. Optional staking and purchases of USD-priced CITY packs in ForgeCoin's Discover Your City game are also future plans. Founders receive a proposed 10% of sale proceeds, not token supply. Exchange functionality remains disabled. See full allocation and bridge conditions.
