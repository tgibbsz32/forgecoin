ForgeCoin (FRG) is a separate, CPU-mined privacy network. Its companion miniFORGE (mFRG) is a proposed standard Solana SPL token with a proposed 500 million fixed-supply Solana token, with 50 million mFRG reserved for staged crypto-denominated exchanges and a disclosed 6% total founder-beneficial allocation. Future FRG privacy improvements include FCMP++ and CARROT, with longer-term L1, L2 and L3 research. The mFRG sale is not yet open.

PROJECT DIRECTION

Private money.
Open participation.

ForgeCoin starts with its own proof-of-work network. The roadmap builds the tools around it: a desktop wallet and miner, an official pool, public monitoring, community representation and future routes to use and exchange FRG.

MONETARY POLICY

A native fair launch

No allocated founder, team or ICO premine. Native block subsidy goes to proof-of-work miners. The primary-emission reference is 50 million FRG, followed by perpetual 0.20 FRG per-block tail emission; there is no fixed maximum supply. Release code and genesis still require independent verification.

MINING & TOOLS

Your choice of mining

Solo mining, the planned official pool with a 1% operator fee, and independent pools. Tauri brings wallet, node and mining controls together; the pool dashboard, standalone Pool Observer and online explorer complete the planned monitoring tools.

DEVELOPMENT & GOVERNANCE

Build the next phase responsibly

Finish native mining infrastructure, prepare miniFORGE distribution and pursue future privacy upgrades. A governance framework has not been chosen; the project intends to establish its structure before an authorized mFRG distribution begins.

DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP

Independent mining. A clear path forward.

Each stage has one purpose. Upcoming token distribution and longer-term privacy research are distinct from the native FRG mining network. See the dedicated mFRG sale and treasury page for the allocation details.

PHASE 1 / NATIVE FRG

Complete and verify mainnet mining

Finish consensus and genesis reviews, independent Windows/Linux/ARM64 tests, safe wallet distribution, RandomX miner integration, the official pool, statistics and explorer. Native FRG remains under public-release restrictions until these checks pass.

PHASE 2 / MINI FORGE DISTRIBUTION

Prepare the staged 50M mFRG fundraising program (5M pilot)

Verify the separate Solana SPL mint, secure custody, sale inventory, purchase terms, compliant eligibility, USDC payment protection and delivery. The sale has not opened; its price and dates are not announced.

AFTER THE SALE / TREASURIES

Liquidity, staking and future development

Reserve 60M mFRG for independently reviewed liquidity, 85M for finite staking participation rewards, 100M for operations, 100M for adoption, 65M for engineering/security and 10M for emergencies. A separate 30M (6%) founder-beneficial allocation has 15M liquid at launch and 15M vesting in months 13–36. No staking yield or liquidity market exists.

BEFORE DISTRIBUTION / GOVERNANCE

Choose and publish governance rules

Establish a governance solution before an authorized mFRG distribution begins. The model, representatives, voting eligibility, treasury approvals and security safeguards remain open for design; no binding governance structure, voting model or bonding requirement has been approved.

FUTURE L1 / PRIVACY

FCMP++ and CARROT

Develop curve-tree storage, full-chain membership proofs, generalized proof verification, CARROT addressing, wallet compatibility and a dedicated stressnet. Prefer a reviewed in-place hard fork if technically safe; activation is not scheduled.

FUTURE L1, L2 & L3 / RESEARCH

Interoperability and private services

Explore native atomic swaps, MPC/TSS controlled settlement, privacy-aware L2 payments and potential L3 merchant applications, marketplaces, auctions and selective-disclosure tools. Each requires independent development and audit.

Development milestones are intended objectives, not guarantees of implementation, dates, returns or availability. mFRG is separate from native FRG and does not provide a fixed conversion rate or claim on FRG reserves. The official pool and full new-mainnet explorer remain subject to independent validation. Read the full technical roadmap.
