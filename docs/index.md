This revises the October 28 500M plan. **Proposed flat presale price: $0.12 per mFRG; sale proceeds 90% project / 10% founders (half founder proceeds immediately, half over six months)**, and no mint, payment, sale or staking operation is authorized. Legacy 500M plans, test fixtures and implementation assumptions require migration before any deployment.

**Ecosystem mission:** following native FRG release verification and published governance procedures, the project intends mFRG as a Solana-side utility and **1 FRG : 1 eligible mFRG reserve-backed bridge** for faster Solana transfers, supported DEX trading, optional audited finite-reserve staking and game payments. No bridge, DEX market, staking or mint is live.

**Discover Your City** is ForgeCoin's planned multiplayer real-world city/property simulation. Optional mFRG checkout for USD-priced CITY packs would require server-side finalized payment verification and exactly-once CITY credit; 100% of mFRG receipts would go to the designated ecosystem treasury. No payment feature is live.

**Native FRG** is mined independently and its 50M primary-emission reference is not a hard cap under continuing tail rewards. The proposed 50M mFRG fixed maximum constrains bridge capacity. Every mFRG presented as FRG-redeemable must have independently verifiable native reserves, including sale/DEX/staking inventory; no current mFRG is verified backed. A 1:1 redemption ratio is not a DEX price peg, and Solana does not inherit native privacy.
