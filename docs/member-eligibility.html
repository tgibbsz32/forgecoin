GOVERNANCE / ELIGIBILITY PREVIEW

Know your voting rights.

ForgeCoin's planned credential system limits each ballot to eligible voters. Explore example profiles to see which constituency elections they would be permitted to enter.

Not a login or registration service. These are fictional demonstration profiles, not verified users. No identity verification, wallet signature, stake snapshot, account creation, ballot acceptance, or persistent storage happens here. Browser controls are not security enforcement.

Example member

The intended production system must verify identity, credentials, election snapshots and wallet signatures server-side or on-chain. Self-selecting a role must never authorize a vote.

Eligibility workflow

  1. Sign a server-issued wallet challenge.
  2. Request one or more constituency credentials.
  3. Submit category-specific evidence privately.
  4. Independent reviewers assess eligibility and appeals.
  5. Snapshot credentials and qualified stake for each election.
  6. Voting authority verifies credentials at ballot submission.

Election permissions

Eligibility may overlap: someone can genuinely be a miner and a developer, but only cast one identity-based ballot per eligible constituency election.
