Historical governance demonstration

This is not an active governance system, voter registry or staking program. Previous fixed-seat chamber, eligibility, bond, registration and vote demos are superseded historical experiments. The project has not selected a replacement governance structure.

The project intends to choose and publish its governance framework before any authorized mFRG distribution begins. The proposed mFRG allocation has since changed to 500M total with a 6% founder-beneficial allocation. No binding elections, registration, voting privileges, treasury execution, staking rewards, wallet signing or identity collection operate here.

Read the current governance overview and the open design charter. Source history remains available through Git.
