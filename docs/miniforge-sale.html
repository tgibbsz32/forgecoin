Governance commitment

Define governance before any distribution begins.

The project intends to publish an approved governance and custody framework before any authorized fundraising distribution begins. There is no approved chamber size, constituency model, token voting system or staking contract yet. The framework must address transparent treasury oversight, conflicts of interest, community participation, proposal approval, key custody and accountability.

Actual treasury transfers, future token releases and any liquidity/staking mechanisms require documented policies, security controls and the appropriate approvals. Holding mFRG does not currently grant voting rights, staking rewards or claims on native FRG.

04 / Common questions

Know the distinction.

Can I buy mFRG now?

No. Mainnet issuance, final price, sale conditions and secure token distribution are still pending. Do not send assets for an unofficial sale.

Is mFRG exchangeable for native FRG at a fixed rate?

No. mFRG and native mined FRG are separate assets. No fixed conversion, redemption guarantee or FRG-backed reserve exists for mFRG.

Is USDC the only future payment option?

USDC on Solana is the initial candidate; SOL and other cryptocurrencies may be added only after separate legal, market, custody, settlement and technical reviews.

Will the liquidity reserve launch immediately?

No. Its separate 60M allocation remains a future plan, while 85M mFRG is separately reserved for proposed finite participation rewards. No DEX trading pair or market value is guaranteed.

Will SOL or USDC balances be private?

No. Solana transactions are publicly auditable. Native FRG privacy properties do not apply to SPL assets or USDC payments.

Where are official updates published?

Follow the miniFORGE token plan, ForgeCoin roadmap, and repository. Ignore unsolicited addresses or sale DMs.
