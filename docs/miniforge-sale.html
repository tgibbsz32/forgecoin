04 / Common questions

Know the distinction.

Can I buy mFRG now?

No. Mainnet issuance, final price, sale conditions and secure token distribution are still pending. Do not send assets for an unofficial sale.

Does mFRG represent one FRG?

No. mFRG is separate from native FRG and separate from a proposed future, fully collateralized wFRG wrapper.

Is USDC the only future payment option?

USDC on Solana is the planned initial checkout currency. Other supported assets may be considered later after separate risk and network reviews.

Will the liquidity reserve launch immediately?

No. Its separate 5M allocation is held for a future decision. No DEX trading pair or market value is guaranteed.

Will SOL or USDC balances be private?

No. Solana transactions are publicly auditable. Native FRG privacy properties do not apply to SPL assets or USDC payments.

Where are official updates published?

Follow the miniFORGE token plan, ForgeCoin roadmap, and repository. Ignore unsolicited addresses or sale DMs.
