Intended 1:1 bridge and utility

ForgeCoin intends mFRG to represent eligible FRG on Solana at a 1 FRG : 1 mFRG fully backed redemption ratio, with faster Solana transfers, supported DEX markets, optional finite-reserve staking and use in ForgeCoin's Discover Your City game. Any presale mFRG claimed as redeemable must already have one independently verified native FRG available in reserve per token; SOL/USDC sale proceeds are not FRG collateral. The 50M SPL supply limits bridge capacity, and a 1:1 backing goal is not a guaranteed trading price.

Governance procedures, full native reserve audits, custody, Solana mint verification, independently reviewed bridging and legal/security approval are prerequisites. No mint, sale, bridge, redemption, staking, liquidity or game payment is active.
