04 / Custody and usage

Separated ledgers.
Verifiable controls.

Development & audits

100M operations and 65M engineering/security, spent against budgets and verified milestones. Proposed 3-of-5 multisignature approval; not active.

Ecosystem grants

100M earmarked for merchant tools, wallet infrastructure and community development, released against documented milestones.

Liquidity

60M mFRG reserved for prospective mFRG/USDC and mFRG/SOL pools. Matching assets, slippage, impermanent loss and withdrawal control require review.

Founder & emergency

15M founder liquid at launch, 15M founder vesting, and 10M emergency restricted reserve. Beneficial owners and authorized signers must be public.

External-asset proceeds

USDC, SOL or other coins exchanged for mFRG belong to a separately controlled treasury and require accounting, security, tax and compliance review.

Privacy-chain connection

Native FRG and Solana mFRG are distinct. No 10:1 peg or automatic FRG backing; future market-based swaps and separate wrapped-FRG designs need audits.

Possible services: merchant checkout, hosted nodes, mining analytics and APIs. An optional 10% burn of **mFRG received from specified paid-service fees** is a proposal, not a transfer tax or a price promise. Native FRG's 50M primary emission is not a hard cap under its documented continuing tail reward.
