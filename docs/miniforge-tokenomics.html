Token structure and stewardship

miniFORGE.
An accountable treasury plan.

A separate standard Solana SPL token, designed with a total target of 50 million mFRG. The initial 5M mFRG sale, separate 5M mFRG post-sale liquidity/staking treasury and 40M mFRG future issuance/development treasury have different purposes and approval needs.

View upcoming mFRG saleRead allocations

mFRG is not native FRG. It is not a wrapped version of native FRG, is not exchangeable for FRG at a fixed rate and does not imply private Solana transfers, share ownership or guaranteed returns.

03 / Governance

Decide the rules before money moves further.

The governance solution will be developed before the proposed mFRG sale concludes. Model, voting eligibility, treasury signers, spending restrictions, oversight, audits, public disclosure and dispute procedures are not yet finalized. There is no active mFRG voting entitlement, staking reward or claim against native FRG holdings.

USDC sale proceeds are separate from the proposed 5M mFRG token liquidity/staking allocation. Any planned use of proceeds will need independent public disclosure and approval.

04 / Important limits

Plans are not live balances.

All token quantities describe proposals. No Solana mainnet mint address, verified token ownership, price, return, liquidity pool or public sale is established by this page. A true 50M fixed cap requires completed supply reconciliation and appropriately revoked mint authority. Solana transactions are public, and native FRG mainnet release remains separately gated.
