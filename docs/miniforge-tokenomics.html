ForgeCoin tokenomics

Native ForgeCoin (FRG) is the primary RandomX-mined network. miniFORGE (mFRG) is a separate, later-stage Solana token proposal.

1. Native ForgeCoin (FRG) — primary network

FRG launches and mines independently of mFRG. Native FRG is the proof-of-work cryptocurrency of the ForgeCoin blockchain. The mFRG sale, Solana mint, and game integrations are not prerequisites for native mining.

Native mainnet status: the selected chain identity is documented, but public launch and live mining are not yet independently verified. Launch requires consensus, miner, wallet, pool and genesis acceptance checks. See current release status.

How FRG enters circulation

FRG is issued through valid proof-of-work blocks, rather than a presale. The subsidy starts above the perpetual tail floor and declines with generated supply. The frozen candidate's estimated height-1 base subsidy is 23.84185222 FRG before transaction fees and applicable consensus adjustments; actual rewards depend on validated chain state.

Emission and long-term security

The primary-emission reference of 50M FRG is not a hard cap: after declining subsidies reach the 0.20 FRG tail floor, tail rewards continue indefinitely to support miner incentives. At a sustained two-minute block interval, the tail equates to approximately 144 FRG per day or 52,560 FRG per 365-day year before transaction fees. This is a steady-state estimate, not a claim of current circulation.

The candidate genesis contains 11.92092895 FRG subject to ordinary maturity; permanent unspendability remains unverified and is a release blocker. Full native consensus and tokenomics specification.

2. miniFORGE (mFRG) — optional, unapproved future proposal

mFRG is not native FRG, and its proposed 50M supply must not be confused with FRG primary emission. mFRG remains a separate later-stage project, without an authorized mint, active sale or guaranteed FRG conversion.

Predeployment only. No independently authenticated Solana mainnet mint, active sale, staking, checkout, bridge or treasury funding. Prior planning figures are obsolete; do not send funds.
