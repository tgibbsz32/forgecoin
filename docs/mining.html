Mining release readiness

The frozen October 26 mainnet has not launched. No public mining, pool endpoint or bootstrap peer is approved. Keep prelaunch mining locked; the retired chain and legacy main-branch binaries are not the new network.

Selected identity: nonce 20261026; P2P/RPC/ZMQ 39480/39481/39482; RandomX, 120-second target; initial difficulty 500,000, minimum 400,000, progressive LWMA90 from height 16. FRG has 8 decimals, a 50M primary reference and perpetual 0.20 FRG tail; no fixed cap.

Native subsidies go to PoW miners. The planned official pool operator fee is 1% of eligible pooled rewards, separate from transaction costs. No pool payouts or accepted public shares are claimed.

November 4 test evidence: the native C pool has accepted a real RandomX block, matured a 99%/1% reward allocation and protected credited balances after a deep test-chain reorganization. A separate native wallet has signed, confirmed and retained a 1 FRG payment after restart. These are isolated FAKECHAIN tests, not actual pool payouts or live-mainnet mining. Before release

Verify matching binaries, genesis treatment, real solo block maturity and compatible pool shares/payouts on authorized disposable infrastructure, independent review, wallet recovery, cross-platform consensus and rollback. Do not mine on the frozen launch chain to test the application.

Possible future merged mining (research only)

FRG + Tari: explore adapting the patched Tari RandomXM proxy so mining on an FRG parent block could also compete for Tari rewards, if Tari accepts FRG-origin RandomX proof and coinbase commitments. This would not import Monero or Tari hashrate into FRG. XMR + FRG: separately research FRG as a Monero-merge-mined auxiliary chain; that would require new audited FRG consensus verification, external miner/pool adoption and a coordinated hard fork. Neither mode is available or promised.

51% risk reduction before launch

Priorities are independent miners and pool operators, geographically diverse nodes and peers, read-only cross-node monitoring of forks and difficulty, and fail-closed pool reward quarantine on deep reorganizations. These controls reduce detection time and settlement exposure; they cannot defeat an attacker with majority work. Read the research, limitations and test gates.
