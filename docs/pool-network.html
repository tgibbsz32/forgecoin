Chain safety and future mining research

Independent node observations and durable deep-reorganization payout holds are being developed and tested; they are not a guarantee against majority-hashpower attacks or evidence of live monitoring. Tari-auxiliary mining alongside FRG and a potential Monero-parent/FRG-auxiliary design are future proposals only, not supported pool modes. The latter requires an audited hard fork.
