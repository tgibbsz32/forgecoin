Worker account and payout threshold

Planned account functionality: register with your payout address and password, review your workers, and request a payout threshold change. Account credentials must be protected by a backend with password hashing, rate limiting, HTTPS and a challenge proving wallet control; entering an arbitrary address alone must not permit payout changes.

Account registration, login and payout-threshold updates are not yet implemented. No credentials are collected or stored on this website.

Address-based private worker accounts

Each future account will be tied to a verified native FRG payout address, a distinct account password and registered worker aliases. Proof of wallet ownership will be required for registration and sensitive changes. Your payout address alone cannot authorize access.

Secure sign-in and account updates are not yet deployed. Registration, login, worker credentials, payout limits and destination changes stay disabled until cryptographic address verification and the audited payout backend are operating.

Future authenticated actions: worker creation and aliases, password changes, address challenge/response recovery, hashrate history, shares and rejections, immature/unpaid/paid balances, payment IDs, changing payout thresholds and requesting changes to a verified payout destination. Destination changes require recent authentication, proof of control of both current and new addresses and an anti-takeover delay.

Get started

  1. Install ForgeCoin Desktop. Open or create a wallet and keep your recovery phrase private.
  2. Choose a mining method. Select Solo to mine through your synchronized local ForgeCoin node, or Pool mining for a compatible Stratum pool.
  3. Wait for the official pool endpoint. No project-operated pool address is published yet. Do not assume Monero pool ports or addresses work for FRG.
  4. For XMRig: install it from its verified upstream source; select RandomX `rx/0`, your FRG payout address, and the verified ForgeCoin Stratum endpoint once announced. Use the pool operator's documented TLS, worker-name and password parameters.
  5. Verify accepted shares. An XMRig process running does not imply shares are accepted. Confirm acknowledgments and subsequent worker statistics.
  6. Check payouts. Only matured, canonical pool rewards are eligible. The final PPLNS window and minimum payout will be published before public mining begins.

Never enter a wallet recovery phrase, private key or wallet password into a pool website.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to use the project pool?

No. Solo mining and third-party pools remain permitted by consensus. The initial project pool is intended as a convenience, not a mining gatekeeper.

How much does the pool charge?

A proposed 1% operator fee on eligible pool rewards. Network transaction costs for payouts will be separately disclosed.

Is the pool limited to 100 miners?

The proposed maximum is 100 simultaneous worker connections, not necessarily 100 distinct people. Actual connection handling and throughput have not yet been load-tested.

What if I see no accepted shares?

Check network identity, correct FRG-compatible endpoint, wallet address, miner RandomX algorithm, TLS settings, connectivity, and rejection logs. A connected miner does not guarantee a valid share.

Why are estimated payouts different from actual payouts?

Mining is probabilistic. Rewards depend on accepted work, pool block discovery, confirmed main-chain blocks, PPLNS eligibility, fee deductions and payout rules.

What happens after another pool operator joins?

Miners may choose independent pools or solo mining. The initial project pool can retire after a public notice and payout reconciliation without changing ForgeCoin consensus.

Why are my account settings unavailable?

GitHub Pages hosts static content. Real registration and payout settings need an independently secured account service and native pool database.
