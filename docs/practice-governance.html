Current FRG governance proposal: 100% active community membership. One eligible member, one nonbinding advisory vote; no separate miner, holder, contributor or node-operator chambers. Membership eligibility and anti-duplicate safeguards remain under development. The founder retains final selection authority during the formative phase, informed by community advice. First advisory voting is targeted no earlier than six months after verified public FRG launch, subject to readiness checks. All rules are proposals and may change. Read the proposed charter.
FRG governance is a proposal, not an active or binding system. Voting weights, eligibility, advisory timelines and future governance models may change as miners, collaborators, contributors, node operators and FRG holders grow. During the founder-led phase, the founder alone selects the governance system to propose and implement, informed by community guidance and advice and focused on the network’s longevity, utility, privacy and resilience. Independent node operators retain control of which consensus rules they run. mFRG remains a separate future proposal. Read the proposed governance charter.

Historical governance demonstration

This is not an active governance system, voter registry or staking program. Previous fixed-seat chamber, eligibility, bond, registration and vote demos are superseded historical experiments. The project has not selected a replacement governance structure.

The project intends to choose and publish its governance framework before any authorized mFRG distribution begins. The proposed mFRG allocation is 50M total: 5M presale, 5M liquidity and 40M combined ecosystem reserve. Founders receive 10% of sale proceeds, not token supply. No binding elections, registration, voting privileges, treasury execution, staking rewards, wallet signing or identity collection operate here.

Read the current governance overview and the open design charter. Source history remains available through Git.
