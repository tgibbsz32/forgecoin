FRG governance is a proposal, not an active or binding system. Voting weights, eligibility, advisory timelines and future governance models may change as miners, collaborators, contributors, node operators and FRG holders grow. During the founder-led phase, the founder alone selects the governance system to propose and implement, informed by community guidance and advice and focused on the network’s longevity, utility, privacy and resilience. Independent node operators retain control of which consensus rules they run. mFRG remains a separate future proposal. Read the proposed governance charter.
